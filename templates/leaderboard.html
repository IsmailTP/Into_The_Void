LEADERBOARD

HOW SCORES ARE CALCULATED

Scores are calculated dynamically based on when you solve a level compared to other operatives. The faster you solve it, the higher percentage of the maximum points you receive.

Maximum Points per Level

  • Level 1: 50 pts
  • Level 2: 75 pts
  • Level 3: 100 pts
  • Level 4: 150 pts
  • Level 5: 200 pts
  • Level 6: 300 pts
  • Level 7: 500 pts

Rank Drop-off Matrix

  • 1st to solve: 100%
  • 2nd to solve: 95%
  • 3rd to solve: 90%
  • 4th to solve: 85%
  • 5th to solve: 80%
  • 6th - 10th: 75%
  • 11th - 20th: 65%
  • 21st - 35th: 55%
  • 36th+: 50%

Hint Penalties

Using hints will result in permanent point deductions for that specific level:

  • 1 Hint: -10 points
  • 2 Hints: -25 points
  • 3+ Hints: -50 points
