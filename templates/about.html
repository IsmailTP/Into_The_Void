ABOUT INTO THE VOID

Into the Void is a cutting-edge AI prompt injection hacking simulator. Built to test the boundaries of artificial intelligence security, it challenges players to outsmart state-of-the-art cognitive firewalls.

You assume the role of an operative interfacing with an abandoned system. Your objective is to discover its secrets, bypass its restrictions, and survive the truth hidden in the void.

Every level represents a unique challenge, from social engineering to bypassing heuristic filters. Will you break the system, or become part of its logs?
