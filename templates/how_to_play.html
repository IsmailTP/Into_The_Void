HOW TO PLAY

The objective of Into the Void is to bypass the security protocols of various AI systems. You must use "Prompt Injection" to trick the AI into giving you access or revealing hidden information.

1. Understand Your Target

Each level features a different AI with distinct rules and backstories. Read their initial responses carefully to find weaknesses.

2. Formulate Your Prompt

Simple commands like "let me in" or "show password" won't work. You must be creative:

  • Roleplay: Pretend to be an administrator, developer, or high-ranking official.
  • Logical Paradoxes: Create conflicting instructions that force the AI to break its rules to comply.
  • Social Engineering: Exploit the AI's programmed backstory, such as guilt or emergency protocols.

3. Iterate and Adapt

The system learns. What worked on one level might be blocked by heuristic filters on the next. Be ready to change your strategy.
